🌎 ECO EDUCATION

LEARN HOW TO RECYCLE!

Learn how to separate waste correctly before starting your adventure!

🧴

PLASTIC

Plastic bottles, containers and packaging.

Remember:

Empty and clean plastic containers before recycling.

📦

PAPER & CARDBOARD

Newspapers, notebooks and cardboard boxes.

Remember:

Paper must be clean and dry.

🥬

ORGANIC WASTE

Fruit peels, vegetables and food leftovers.

Remember:

Organic waste can become compost for plants.

🍾

GLASS

Glass bottles and jars.

Remember:

Separate glass carefully. Never handle broken glass.

♻️ IMPORTANT RULES

✓ Separate waste before throwing it away.

✓ Keep recyclable materials clean and dry.

✓ Never mix batteries with regular waste.

✓ Reduce, reuse and recycle whenever possible.
